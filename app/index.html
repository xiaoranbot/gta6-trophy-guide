Platinum estimate: around 200 hours. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals — long, not hard.

Save before every chapter

GTA VI has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

1 confirmed missable: Positive Mental Attitude — Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward it and disappears after that chapter. Do it during Chapter 1. (Source: Output Lag trophy guide.)

7 trophies have no confirmed unlock method yet (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin). Their guides say so plainly and only use the official description as a hint — nothing is guessed.
